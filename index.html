The Lore

One lazy afternoon in heaven, Moe the cupid was busy lounging around when she accidentally knocked over the Book of Fates. Papers were everywhere! And in her panic she shoved them back in any order... Accidentally scrambling the soulmates. Whoops!

Now people who were never supposed to cross paths are now inexplicably tied together. Enemies? Soulmates. Rivals? Lovers. Complete opposites? Well, let's see how that goes.

If fate says they shouldn't be together, that's probably exactly what we're looking for.

The open collab is for Janitor AI creators who want to play with those scrambled pairings. Make a bot with an “opposites attract” trope where the couple was never meant to work, but did. Tag it #MISSedMatch and swipe through the matches below.

Note: Only bots with the tag, uses banner within the sourcecode + promotes in Velvet Hour can be featured on the site
